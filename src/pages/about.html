About BACK TO HOME

Global resources.
A stronger Kerala.

A community built to help expat Malayalis return home with purpose, and to grow a new culture of work and enterprise in Kerala.

Our Vision

Uniting global Malayali talent to make Kerala a model state for the world.

Our Mission

A global platform that connects, empowers and engages

To create a global platform that connects, empowers and engages Malayalis worldwide, enabling them to contribute their talent, knowledge, experience and resources towards transforming Kerala into a model state for the world.

  • Connect: Malayalis across the world with each other and with Kerala
  • Empower: with guidance, training, mentors and opportunity
  • Engage: talent, knowledge and resources in Kerala's growth
Why it matters

Not just encouragement. A thoughtful transition.

Our goal isn't only to motivate people to return. It's to make that change thoughtful and genuinely rewarding.

Every expat's background, financial situation, family responsibilities and aspirations are different. So instead of offering one solution for everyone, we look at each person's needs.

Our broader goals are to use the valuable experience and knowledge gained abroad for Kerala's progress, to grow entrepreneurship, to create jobs, and to strengthen our homeland's social and economic growth.

Who Is Eligible to Join BACK TO HOME?
Any expat Malayali who is planning or considering a return to Kerala: employees, professionals, aspiring entrepreneurs, investors, and returnees who want to grow an existing venture.
Do I Need to Return to Kerala Before Joining?
No. Our programs are designed so you can prepare while still working abroad. Most sessions are online, with optional in-person meet-ups in Kerala.
I Don't Have a Business Idea Yet. Can I Still Join?
Yes. The Homecoming Blueprint starts with self-assessment and opportunity mapping, so you can find the path that suits you: a job, a business, a partnership or an investment.
Does BACK TO HOME Guarantee Employment or Business Success?
No one can honestly guarantee that. What we offer is structured guidance, feasibility support, mentors and a network that greatly improve your chances of a successful, well-planned return.
How Much Investment Will I Need?
It depends on your chosen path. Some members go into employment or consulting with no investment at all. We help you match opportunities to your actual investment capacity.
How Can I Apply?
Fill in the application form. Our team will review your profile and schedule a short discovery call to recommend the right program.
