A community built to help expat Malayalis return home with purpose, and to grow a new culture of work and enterprise in Kerala.

Our Vision

Uniting global Malayali talent to make Kerala a model state for the world.

Our Mission

A global platform that connects, empowers and engages

To create a global platform that connects, empowers and engages Malayalis worldwide, enabling them to contribute their talent, knowledge, experience and resources towards transforming Kerala into a model state for the world.

  • Connect: Malayalis across the world with each other and with Kerala
  • Empower: with guidance, training, mentors and opportunity
  • Engage: talent, knowledge and resources in Kerala's growth
Why it matters

Not just encouragement. A thoughtful transition.

Our goal isn't only to motivate people to return. It's to make that change thoughtful and genuinely rewarding.

Every expat's background, financial situation, family responsibilities and aspirations are different. So instead of offering one solution for everyone, we look at each person's needs.

Our broader goals are to use the valuable experience and knowledge gained abroad for Kerala's progress, to grow entrepreneurship, to create jobs, and to strengthen our homeland's social and economic growth.
