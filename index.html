About the initiative

More than a dream of returning. A plan for it.

For almost every Malayali who has spent years abroad, going home is a deep wish. But worries about jobs, business opportunities, financial stability and the family's future often hold that wish back.

BACK TO HOME exists to close the gap between that dream and reality. Life abroad has given you valuable work experience, skills, knowledge, confidence and financial resources. We believe these can become the foundation for new opportunities in Kerala, and help build a new culture of work and enterprise back home.

  • Discover opportunities that match your skills and investment capacity
  • Get guidance, training and feasibility support before you move
  • Connect with entrepreneurs, experts and institutions in Kerala

FAQ

Questions you may have

Can't find what you're looking for? Send us an enquiry and our team will get back to you.

Send an enquiry
Who can join BACK TO HOME?
Any expat Malayali who is planning or considering a return to Kerala: employees, professionals, aspiring entrepreneurs, investors, and returnees who want to grow an existing venture.
Do I need to return to Kerala before joining?
No. Our programs are designed so you can prepare while still working abroad. Most sessions are online, with optional in-person meet-ups in Kerala.
I don't have a business idea yet. Can I still join?
Yes. The Homecoming Blueprint starts with self-assessment and opportunity mapping, so you can find the path that suits you: a job, a business, a partnership or an investment.
Do you guarantee a job or business success?
No one can honestly guarantee that. What we offer is structured guidance, feasibility support, mentors and a network that greatly improve your chances of a successful, well-planned return.
How much investment do I need?
It depends on your chosen path. Some members go into employment or consulting with no investment at all. We help you match opportunities to your actual investment capacity.
How do I apply?
Fill in the application form. Our team will review your profile and schedule a short discovery call to recommend the right program.
